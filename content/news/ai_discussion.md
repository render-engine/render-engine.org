title: A Discussion on AI Contributions to Render Engine
---
While Render Engine currently accepts AI assisted contributions, I believe that the time has
come for a discussion on whether we should continue to. If you are a maintainer, contributor,
or user of Render Engine please go to the [discussion thread] and let us know what your
thoughts are on the issue. I will leave the issue open for discussion through December 4, 2026
with a decision published by December 7, 2026.

[discussion thread]: https://github.com/render-engine/render-engine/issues/1357
